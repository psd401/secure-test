Help for teachers

What you can do, and how

Secure Test locks a student's Mac for the length of a test. This tool is where you build that test, run the session, watch it live, score it and report on it. Each topic below is a few steps and a picture; start at the top or jump to what you need.

Topics

    1

    Get started

    Sign in with your school Google account. Everything you own, or that is shared with you, is on one page.

    1. Open the app and press Sign in with Google. Use your school account.
    2. You land on Assessments: each row shows its status (Draft or Published), its test sessions and a Results button.
    3. Assessments a colleague shared with you sit under Shared with you until you press Add to my assessments.
    4. Your class lists come from PowerSchool and refresh every morning; you never type a roster.

    Anything odd? Send feedback, top right of every page, reaches the project team with the page you were on.

    2

    Build an assessment

    Start blank, add questions of any type, and check it the way a student will see it.

    1. On the home page press New assessment and give it a name.
    2. Press Add question and choose a type: multiple choice (one or several answers), short answer, essay, matching, ordering, hotspot on a picture, drawing, or a table.
    3. Write the question and its answer key. Math goes between dollar signs, for example $\frac{1}{2}$, and shows as you type.
    4. Upload pictures on the Images page (link at the top of the home page), then add them to a question.
    5. Press Show preview to see exactly what a student will see, without the lockdown.
    3

    Bring in an existing test

    Most tests already exist on paper. Let the tool read them, then check what it found.

    1. Inside an assessment press Import items from PDF and choose the file. Scanned pages work too.
    2. The tool proposes questions, answer keys and reading passages. Nothing is added until you accept it.
    3. Check each proposal, fix what it got wrong, and add the ones you want.
    4. A colleague's exported test? Use Import assessment file on the home page.
    Looks wrong but isn't: a source marked "Looks shorter than the document — check it" means the tool thinks it missed text. Compare it with the paper before adding.
    4

    Readings and sources

    Put a passage, or several labelled sources, next to the questions that use them.

    1. Add a stimulus: the passage or sources, with any charts, shared by one or more questions.
    2. Choose its layout. Side by side puts the sources beside the question and is the default whenever there are sources.
    3. In Settings, set How students move through the test to One question at a time. Side by side needs it.
    4. Students can switch between Beside and Above themselves, and click a chart to enlarge it.
    Looks wrong but isn't: sources above the question instead of beside it usually means the test is set to one scrolling page, or the student's window is narrow.
    5

    Accommodations

    Each student's documented supports, such as contrast, font and zoom, follow them into every test.

    1. Open Students. Your class list is already there from PowerSchool.
    2. Press Import TIDE settings to bring in the state testing accommodations file.
    3. Open a student to review or change their supports. Your edits survive the next TIDE import.
    4. An assessment's Accommodations tab narrows which supports apply to that test. It saves as you go.
    6

    Publish and practise

    Only a Published test can be given. Try it yourself, locked down, before your class does.

    1. Press Publish. A Published test is locked so a running test can't change; Unpublish to edit.
    2. Open Test sessions and press Practice on my Mac. A practice session opens for you alone for the rest of the day.
    3. Open Secure Test on your Mac, sign in, and find the test under Your tests. It is the real thing: full screen, locked, timed.
    4. Back in the tool, See my answers shows how you did; Practice again starts over.

    Practice never appears in your results, Monitor or printouts, and is deleted a week later. It needs Secure Test on your own Mac.

    7

    Run a test session

    Open the test for your class, watch it live, and decide when it ends.

    1. In Test sessions, choose who it is for (all your sections, one section, or picked students) and how long it stays open, then press Start session.
    2. Students see the test under Your tests right away. The six-letter Session code is for anyone whose list is empty.
    3. Monitor shows every student: Not joined, In progress, Idle, Needs attention, Handed in. View screen shows one student's screen once (they are told you looked).
    4. Need more time? Adjust time for everyone, for one student, or for students you tick. No time limit removes it.
    5. Close session when you're done, then Hand in everyone to finalise work still in progress.

    Three clocks: what ends a student's test

    Time limit

    Set in Settings. Counts per student from when they open the test. At zero their Mac ends the session; answers are kept.

    Stops the student

    Session length or Close

    When the session runs out or you close it, students return to Your tests with answers saved. They can resume in a later session.

    Stops the student

    Hand in

    The student's Finish and hand in, or yours on the Monitor. Nothing is scored until work is handed in.

    Finalises the work
    8

    Score and give feedback

    Questions with answer keys score themselves. Essays and hand-scored questions wait for you, with help from AI if you want it.

    1. On an essay question, Upload rubric (PDF, Word, Markdown or pasted text) or reuse one from your library.
    2. Open the Scoring queue. Each answer waits there with its rubric.
    3. Press Score with AI for a proposed score and feedback per criterion. Approve it, change it, or score it yourself.
    4. The question's scoring method decides what happens next: with AI, every AI score waits for your approval; with Hybrid, a confident AI score becomes final on its own and the rest wait for you.
    Safeguarding alerts: handed-in writing is screened for signs that a student may be at risk, and for attempts to trick the AI scorer. An alert appears on your home page beside the assessment. Follow your school's usual process for a student at risk; the alert does not replace it.
    9

    Results and printing

    One row per student, one column per question, and several ways to put it on paper.

    1. Results shows the class, with a section filter, totals and percent. Download CSV takes it to a spreadsheet.
    2. Open a student for their answers, scores and a timeline of their session.
    3. Print report gives a class summary, or a single family-facing page for one student.
    4. Print student work prints each student's answers, optionally anonymous with a key page, for marking by hand.
    5. Pass back returns a handed-in test to a student to keep working; earlier scores are kept as a record.
    10

    Share and co-teach

    Give a colleague their own copy, or work on the same test with a co-teacher.

    1. Press Share and enter a colleague's school email. They get their own copy; later edits don't travel either way.
    2. For a class you teach together, use Co-teach in the same dialog. You both see and run the same test and its results.
    3. Duplicate makes your own Draft copy, for a second period's version or before a big edit.

    Things that look wrong but aren't

    A student's row still says In progress after I closed the session.
    Closing returns students to Your tests with answers saved, but does not hand in. Press Hand in on the row, or open another session and they resume.
    A row says Idle while the student is clearly writing.
    Idle means nothing has been saved for 10 minutes. View screen shows what they are doing; typed text saves every few seconds on current Macs.
    A student's session ended by itself.
    Their time limit ran out ("Time is up."), or the session was closed or ran out. Their answers are kept either way.
    "No tests assigned right now."
    No session is open for a section that student is in today. Read them the Session code.
    "This Mac isn't set up for Secure Test yet."
    That Mac hasn't received its settings from IT yet. Contact IT, not the project.
    Wi-Fi drops mid-test.
    Answers are stored on the Mac and sent when the connection returns.
    Delete is greyed out.
    A Published test must be unpublished first; a test with student work can only be archived.
