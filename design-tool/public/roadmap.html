Secure Test
Peninsula School District

From a one-day experiment to a district pilot

Secure Test locks a student's Mac for the length of a test, and gives teachers a web tool to build, run, monitor and score those tests. It is built in the open by the district's Research & Assessment office.

Every stop is a real date. ★ marks the high points. The rest shows how each one was reached: small steps, each checked by hand before the next.

Order, not dates. This is the planned sequence as of the date below. Your input can change it: use Suggest a change on any stop, or Send feedback at the top. Feedback needs a PSD staff sign-in.

    How work moves

    • Teacher tool changes go live within the hour.
    • Student app changes need a signed release, then reach district Macs the next day through IT.
    • Every change is tried by hand in a real session before it is called done.
